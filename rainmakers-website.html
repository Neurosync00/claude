OVERALL CONCLUSION — ROBINSON DEEP

The City of Johannesburg's Flagship Waste Diversion Initiative

The Robinson Deep AWTT Project is the City of Johannesburg's flagship waste diversion initiative. Under Synergyworx Rainmakers' PFDM model, the project will transform Robinson Deep from a conventional landfill — projected to reach capacity by June 2024 without intervention — into an integrated recycling and waste-to-energy facility, processing up to 500,000 tonnes of waste annually, generating renewable electricity, extending landfill life, and providing a permanent long-term solution to Johannesburg's landfill airspace crisis.

Robinson Deep will serve as Johannesburg's — and South Africa's — proof of concept for the Municipal Circular Intelligence Economy: a model replicable across every metro in the country and across Africa, positioning the City of Johannesburg as a global leader in circular economy infrastructure delivery.
